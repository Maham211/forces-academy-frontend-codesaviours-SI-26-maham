ABOUT FORCES ACADEMY

Empowering Candidates for Pakistan's Armed Forces

Established in Faisalabad, Forces Academy is a premier training institute specializing in initial computer tests, ISSB psychological tests, outdoor obstacle courses, and interview preparation.

Guided by retired Officers and Senior Psychologists, we provide state-of-the-art computerized testing labs and physical training grounds designed to mirror official military selection centers.
